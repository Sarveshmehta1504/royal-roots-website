PLYWOOD BASICS
BWP stands for Boiling Waterproof, and is certified to IS 710, the highest moisture resistance grade in India. Royal Roots Platinum grade is BWP certified, and it is ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, marine applications, and any extreme moisture area.
BWR meets IS 303 and resists moisture under normal conditions while BWP meets IS 710 and withstands boiling water. Silver and Gold are BWR and Platinum is BWP.
We offer Silver (BWR IS 303, 15-year warranty), Gold (Enhanced BWR IS 303, 25-year warranty), and Platinum (BWP IS 710, 30-year warranty).
We manufacture and supply 6mm, 9mm, 12mm, 16mm, 18mm, and 19mm. 18mm and 19mm are optimal for structural verticals and cabinets, while thinner sheets are ideal for paneling and backing.
BUYING AND ORDERING

Quality you can verify.

Our products undergo rigorous testing to ensure they meet the highest national standards for durability and safety.

IS 710 BWP

This is India's highest plywood standard requiring the board to withstand 72 hours of boiling water immersion without delaminating. Royal Roots Platinum grade carries this certification ensuring absolute moisture resistance.

IS 303 BWR

The rigorous standard for Moisture Resistant and Boiling Water Resistant grade plywood. Perfect for long-lasting interior furniture applications with high structural integrity.

ISO 9001:2015

Certified for robust quality management systems. This ensures our manufacturing and service delivery processes remain consistently exceptional at every stage.
